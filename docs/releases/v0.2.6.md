## FM27 Trainer 0.2.6 — Cleaner look, auto-read, extra Game Pass fallback

### New
- **A cleaner, smoother interface.** New colours, ring gauges for the four components, bars that glide to their
  value, and soft transitions between pages and on buttons.
- **Auto-read.** A switch on the Overview page re-reads the Breakdown every few seconds (5 to 120) while it is
  open in FM. It never brings FM to the front, only takes over a complete reading, and leaves your values alone
  when nothing changed. Off by default.

### Fixed
- **Game Pass: one more way to find your manager.** If the usual two ways fail on a Game Pass build, the app now
  also looks in the game's list of human managers. This has not been live-tested by us yet.

### Thanks
- The Game Pass fallback and the idea for auto-read come from arhxmmmmm's fork (forked-fm27-trainer), who also
  supplied a test for the grey empty gauge segments Game Pass draws. That test passes on the reader from 0.2.4.
