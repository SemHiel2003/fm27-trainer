## FM27 Trainer 0.2.2 — Tactical understanding, screen reading on any screen

### New
- **Maximise tactical understanding.** The Squad page shows how well your squad knows the current tactic, per area
  (Passing Style, Build Up, Chance Creation, In Possession Shape, Line of Engagement, Pressing Intensity, Pressing
  Trap, Out Of Possession Shape) and per player, and one click puts everything at 100 %, like FM's in-game editor
  does. Undo takes it back. Players who have never trained with the team yet are skipped until they have.
  Windows only for now; on macOS the record is looked up at the expected place and the button does nothing if
  it is not found.

### Fixed
- **Screen reading on smaller screens and other UI scales.** Several people saw "Read 8 of 17 gauges" with key
  factors stuck at 0 or 50. On a 1080p or smaller screen the pies are only 12–18 px across and the OCR misreads
  labels. The reader now works at a fixed reference size (the capture is rescaled when the text is not about
  20 px high), matches labels tolerantly, derives rows the OCR missed from the row spacing and the "Key Factors"
  headers, measures each pie after enlarging it, and reads the component bars as tick peaks with the frame border
  excluded. Tested with a 2560×1440 capture scaled from 1366×768 up to 4K: all 17 gauges and 4 bars identical.

### Notes
- Writes still only happen on the FM build that was live-tested (Steam 26340721, November 2026); after an FM
  patch the app is read-only until the new build has been checked, and you can allow changes in Settings.
- The macOS DMG is built on a Mac with `./release.sh`; the Windows zip comes from GitHub Actions.
