## FM27 Trainer 0.2.4 — Connects again after FM's release updates; screen reading in every FM language

### Fixed
- **"Not connected" / "No human manager with a club found" since 10 November.** FM27's full release (10 November)
  and the update of 12 November moved the game's internal lists, and the app only knew the build of 2 November. It
  now knows Steam builds 26385295 and 26577676 (Windows and Mac) and Game Pass 0.9.2435.0.
- **Future FM updates no longer break the connection.** On a build the app does not know yet, it now finds the
  game's lists by itself, so reading club, squad, calendar and date keeps working right after a patch. Changes stay
  off on such a build until you allow them in Settings (back up your save first).
- **Changes are on for the new builds**: Steam 26577676 on Apple silicon Macs was live-tested (reading, budgets,
  morale, training, each with undo). The Windows builds (Steam and Game Pass) follow FMLibreEditor's measurement
  that the game's object layout did not change; every write is still checked against the game's memory first.
  Keep a backup of your save. On Intel Macs the app is read-only until you allow changes in Settings.
- **Mac download.** The Mac version was missing from 0.2.2 and 0.2.3, so the download link gave a 404. It is back.
- **FM in another language.** Reading the Breakdown only knew the English labels, so with FM in German, Spanish,
  Dutch, Turkish or any other language nothing was recognised. The app now knows the Breakdown labels of every
  language FM27 offers (taken from FM's own language files) and finds the language by itself. FM in Chinese,
  Japanese, Korean, Russian or Greek also needs Windows to read that script: add the language with *Optical
  character recognition* under Settings → Time & language → Language & region. Arabic is not supported yet.
- **US English.** "Coaching Support" and "Head Coach Tendencies" are now recognised.
- **"Read 8 of 17 gauges", key factors at 0.** A letter of a label or the small icon in front of it could be taken
  for a gauge and read as empty (0). A gauge must now be a full round disc, and a reading of 0 is always flagged
  for you to check. When the text recognition glued a gauge to the end of its label (it reads the gauge as an
  "O"), or a long label touched its gauge, the app looked in the wrong place; that is fixed too.
- **The four blocks at 90–100.** On a darker capture (for example with HDR on) the frame around each bar was
  counted as part of the scale, which put every bar at 90 or 100. The scale is now recognised by its even steps,
  and the coloured marker by being a narrow stripe. A block is also read when its title was not recognised.
- **Darker, lighter and washed-out captures.** What counts as the light, empty part of a gauge and as its colour
  now depends on the background around it instead of fixed values, and a washed-out fill (a typical HDR
  screenshot) still counts. Gauges are no longer read one segment too high or too low on such captures, and a
  strong club colour behind the panels is not taken for a gauge.
- **The wrong window.** The app took the largest window with "Football Manager" in its title. A browser with a
  page about FM open could be that window. On Windows it now takes FM's own window (fm.exe), and never a browser.
- **Windows without text recognition for its own language** (for example Thai): the app now falls back to any
  other language Windows can read, such as English, and tries every installed language when hardly anything was
  recognised. On the Mac it tries the languages macOS can read (from the next Mac build).

### For reports
- Every reading saves `last-analysis.txt` next to `last-breakdown.png` in the app folder (Settings → Open app
  folder): what the app read, gauge by gauge, and all recognised text. If a reading is incomplete or something
  is flagged, the app asks you to send both files. Without them a wrong reading cannot be reproduced.
